# Disclaimer

Financial Decision Studio is a **financial planning and educational tool**. Please read this before relying on its output.

## Not a forecast or guarantee

Every projection, chart, and metric the application produces is the mechanical result of the assumptions you (or the built-in defaults) enter — expected returns, inflation, income growth, mortgage terms, tax parameters, and so on. Changing an assumption changes the output. Nothing the application shows is a prediction, promise, or guarantee about your actual financial future.

## Not personalized advice

Financial Decision Studio does not know your complete financial, legal, tax, or personal situation. It does not provide:

- personalized investment advice,
- personalized tax advice or tax return preparation,
- personalized legal advice, or
- personalized insurance advice.

The built-in tax assumptions (including any country-specific "planning pack," such as the Italy 2026 pack referenced in the user guide) are simplified planning approximations, not tax-filing software, and should not be used to prepare or file an actual tax return.

## Monte Carlo, sensitivity, and robustness tools

Probabilistic tools (Monte Carlo simulation, sensitivity analysis, robustness checks) describe how outcomes vary across a modeled range of scenarios given your assumptions. They are decision-support aids for understanding uncertainty and fragility — they are not statements of the probability that any particular real-world outcome will occur.
